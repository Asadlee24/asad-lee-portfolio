Who is Asad Lee?

I am an independent Cyber Security Researcher and Full-Stack Engineer who builds software where security and intuitive design reinforce each other. Over the last 4+ years, I have engineered decentralized systems, high-throughput web applications, and mission-critical payment workflows.
